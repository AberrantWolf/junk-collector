# Junk Collector v0.1.0-beta.21

Audio uses the normal signed-in website connection by default, including through
proxies and tunnels. Login and collection permissions always apply. Collector
does not detect providers or enforce their restrictions.

Use **Administration → Server settings → Audio connection → Require a separate
audio connection** to opt into private or separate HTTPS playback. The saved
choice persists across restart and overrides the `remote_media_allowed` config
default. Existing explicit `false` configurations retain their separate-route
choice. Alternate endpoints may remain configured while normal audio is selected.
Reconnect other clients to clear cached routes after changing the setting.

Cancelled or withdrawn processing publications now remove their own unpublished
journal/staging before releasing the archive writer. This prevents that abandoned
work from blocking a later publication or restart. Existing conflicting or unknown
journals are retained for inspection; no blanket historical cleanup is performed.
Raw masters, manual metadata, accepted sources and unknown module data are preserved.

This prerelease advances `preview` and leaves stable `latest` unchanged. Stop
writers and retain a matching complete Fi private-state/NAS archive recovery pair
before updating. Install the exact qualified image. The remote-archive Compose
profile retains its provisioned media authority even when normal audio is selected,
so a later toggle can enable the alternate route and a fresh media volume can start.

See [audio behavior and review](../validation/audio-connection-policy-2026-11-04.md)
and the [release operation record](../validation/container-beta21-publication-2026-11-04.md).
