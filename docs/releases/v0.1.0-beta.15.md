# Junk Collector v0.1.0-beta.15

> Deployment identifiers are anonymized; dates, source identities, outcomes and qualification limits are unchanged. Example addresses are not deployment instructions.

This beta completes integer PCM WAV digital-release support and includes the
private email purchase inbox and Proton Bridge connection/recovery work delivered
since beta.14. Collector core and media update together; Trash Amp is unchanged.

## WAV digital originals

Import WAV originals without creating physical copies. Exact source bytes remain
preserved. Explicit FLAC preparation retains sample rate, channels and bit depth,
with independent sample comparison before publication. Existing FLAC and MP3
originals play directly; MP3 is never reencoded.

WAV INFO/ID3 tags and embedded covers feed normal metadata/artwork. Explicit album
matching reads original PCM without an encoder and honors excluded alternates.
Existing retained digital packages can be analyzed in place from the Music editor;
manual edits, originals, Captures and physical inventory survive.

Prepared Music FLAC downloads carry accepted album/track metadata, available cover
preview and descriptive filenames without modifying archived listening files.
Saved download links retain authorized historical audio access when its owning
module becomes unavailable.

Supported WAV inputs are RIFF/checked extensible integer PCM, 16/24-bit mono/stereo,
8–192 kHz, at most 24 hours/file and 4,096 chunks. Float/compressed WAV, multichannel,
RF64 and continuous-image/CUE splitting remain unsupported. Audio matching requires
configured providers; identity candidates do not establish catalog verification.
See [workflow](../digital-purchases.md) and
[validation](../validation/wav-digital-completion-2026-11-01.md).

## Private purchase inbox and Bridge

Review private email purchase suggestions, accept/link them deliberately, dismiss,
restore and reopen them for correction. Proton Bridge setup, pinned connections,
sync status, scanned-folder editing and credential revalidation are available in
the web UI. Durable receipts, owner/cancellation fences and interrupted-work
recovery preserve accepted purchases and connection history. This does not enable
scanning or replace saved credentials automatically. The installation bundle includes
the optional mail override, Bridge build context and local provisioning helper. See
[email purchase support](../email-purchases.md).

## Upgrade and rollback

Use `ghcr.io/aberrantwolf/junk-collector:v0.1.0-beta.15` or the immutable digest in
`release-image.json`. Update core and media together, retaining existing Compose,
configuration, ServerId/TLS, accounts, grants, Jobs, imports, scratch, archive and
Bridge state. Do not rerun setup or initialize a replacement archive. Existing
beta.14 installations require no new enrollment or destructive audio conversion.
First adoption from beta.13 or earlier still requires the documented offline
[archive enrollment](../../deploy/container/README.md#update).

Pull before downtime, stop all writers, and retain a complete consistent recovery
point before activation. application-host's local state/config and remote archive require a
matching stopped-writer recovery pair; preserve the old image and stack definition.
Rollback after new writes restores both members of that pair with all writers
stopped, rather than downgrading only the image or database. See
[snapshot recovery](../../deploy/container/snapshot-recovery.md) and the
[remote archive runbook](../../deploy/container/remote-archive.md).
This prerelease advances `preview`; stable `latest` remains unchanged.
